# Product Research Evidence

## Status and authority

This directory preserves external research and market evidence that materially informs Lunowa Product reasoning.

Research artifacts are **evidence inputs, not Product authority by themselves**. `docs/product/PRODUCT.md` owns the current Product-level synthesis and must distinguish accepted internal direction from external evidence, inference, hypothesis, and unknowns.

Rules:

- prefer primary sources, peer-reviewed research, official product documentation, and direct behavioral evidence;
- record publication/retrieval date when time sensitivity matters;
- do not convert competitor feature absence into a durable differentiation claim without current verification;
- do not convert laboratory cognitive findings into direct evidence of email-market demand;
- do not convert vendor marketing telemetry into independent causal evidence;
- do not treat a research artifact as proof of ICP, switching, willingness to pay, or retention for Lunowa;
- preserve material counter-evidence and limitations, not only evidence favorable to the current thesis;
- when evidence materially changes Product direction, update `docs/product/PRODUCT.md` in the same accepted change where practical.
