# Decision Record

Use when a decision is long-lived, costly to reverse, security/architecture significant, or likely to be misunderstood later. Do not create one for every local implementation choice.

## Status

Proposed / Accepted / Superseded / Rejected

## Date

YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem, constraint, or decision point required a choice?

## Decision

What was decided?

## Rationale

Why is this choice appropriate under the current evidence and constraints?

## Alternatives considered

List credible alternatives and the key reason each was not selected.

## Consequences

Positive and negative consequences, including maintenance, security, reliability, cost, lock-in, migration, or operational implications where material.

## Revisit triggers

What evidence or change in constraints should cause this decision to be reconsidered?

## Supersedes / superseded by

Link related records when applicable.
